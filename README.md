# Audit Reports

Independent security reviews of EVM protocols by Imran Khadri.

Each report includes the vulnerable code, the attack path, impact analysis,
a runnable Foundry proof of concept, and a recommended fix.

## Published

| Date | Protocol | Severity | Report |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | First report pending coordinated disclosure | — | — |

## Method

Reports follow a fixed structure: scope and commit, severity with rationale,
the defect, how funds are lost, why it cannot be recovered, a walkthrough,
a runnable PoC, impact, and the suggested fix. Findings are checked against
prior audits and published known issues before submission.
